Leek & bacon quiche

Silky custard, sweet leeks and a crisp, buttery crust.

Servings
6

Ingredients

Shortcrust pastry1 sheet · 230 g
Leeks2 · 300 g
Bacon lardons200 g
Large eggs3 · 150 g
Heavy cream300 ml
Show 4 more

Steps

1 · Preheat and line

Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Line a 23 cm (9 in) tart pan with 1 sheet of shortcrust pastry (230 g) and prick the base.

2 · Blind bake

Bake the empty crust for 12 minutes until pale golden.
