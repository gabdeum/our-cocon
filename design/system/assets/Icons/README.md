# Icons

Line icons on a 24 × 24 grid: stroke 2.2 (2.4–2.6 for back, chevron, close and plus), round caps and joins, drawn in ink `#2A2414`. Recolour in code (on-ink in the tab bar, olive in the timer pill, white on olive). Names say what they mean: home, calendar, recipes, groceries, menu (tab bar); settings; person (Others in the calendar); car (who takes someone, in the day view); back, chevron-right, chevron-down and chevron-up (unit pickers), close, plus, search, filter, share, more, more-vertical (a grocery row's menu); timer, thermometer, screen-on (cooking); check, trash, camera, message, mail, swap (variant change), view-only, warning, play.
